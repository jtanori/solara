## Section brief: Transits — the year's dates that matter

This section reads the key transits to the natal chart during the solar return year: the calendar inside the sky.

Cover:
- Each named transit in the digest (`chart.transits`): transiting body, natal point, the aspect, and the dates or window the digest gives. Lead with the slow bodies and anything touching the natal Sun, Moon, or angles.
- For each: one sentence of what it asks and one sentence of when to lean in versus when to rest — grounded in the dates given, never invented.
- The overall rhythm: is this a year of long slow background transits or punctuated events?

Shape: one prose block for the rhythm, one `list` block with one item per transit (each item names bodies, aspect, window, meaning in a line), one closing prose block. A `closingQuestion` is optional here.
