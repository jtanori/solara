## Section brief: The Planetary Year — the year's ruling lord

This section reads the profection-derived year lord: the planet that holds the year's chair.

Cover:
- The profection age and its sign and house, and the year lord the digest names (`chart.profections`) — what it means that this planet, of all of them, runs this year.
- How the year lord's own condition in the return chart (its sign and house in `chart.bodies`, its tightest aspects in `chart.aspects` / `chart.crossAspects`) qualifies its rule.
- One devotion: a small, repeatable act that courts the year lord.

Shape: 2 prose blocks, one callout with the devotion. A `closingQuestion` is optional here.
