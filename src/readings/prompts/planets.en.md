## Section brief: Planets — the cast of the year

This section tours the planets in the solar return chart as a cast of characters the reader works with this year.

Cover:
- Each planet's sign and whole-sign house in the return chart (the `chart.bodies` array), grouped meaningfully (personal planets, social planets, outer planets) rather than as a dry inventory.
- Two standouts: the bodies in the tightest cross-aspects to the natal chart (`chart.crossAspects`) or on the return angles get a longer paragraph each — why they dominate the year.
- What changes when this cast, rather than the natal cast, runs the show.

Shape: one prose block for the cast, one `keyValue` block (key = planet with sign and house, value = one line of this-year meaning), one prose block for the standouts. A `closingQuestion` is optional here.
