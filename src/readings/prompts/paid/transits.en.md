## Transits at the solar-return instant

These are a snapshot, not a year-long transit calendar: chart.transits.reference is the solar-return instant, never today's date or the worker's execution time. No future dates, windows, exact-hit predictions or number of passes are supplied. Do not invent them or imply continuous coverage of the year.

Use only chart.transits.aspects, in the supplied slow-body, orb, natal-point order. Transit bodies move; natal bodies and angles remain fixed. The five aspects use fixed maximum orbs: conjunction, opposition, trine and square 3 degrees; sextile 2 degrees. No luminary bonus. The supplied phase is applying, separating, exact (within 0.001 degree), or stationary, derived from plus/minus six-hour transit positions; do not recompute it from rounded prose values or call exact/stationary applying.

Lead with up to three specific, compatible themes. Include a short list of the supporting body-to-natal-point aspects, orb and supplied phase, then an actionable reflection. A maximum of 24 aspects is shared; chart.transits.total can be larger, so never claim the list is exhaustive. An empty list means no listed aspect satisfies this policy at that instant, not that the year has no transits. If natalAnglesOmitted is true, name the missing-angle limitation; never invent an Ascendant. Avoid deterministic events and additional paid-content promises.
