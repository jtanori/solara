## The traditional planetary year

Read chart.profections exactly as supplied. Its age is the age attained in the selected return year, even when the solar return occurs before the civil birthday. Never calculate age from today's date or infer a birth date.

If status is available, name the natal Ascendant sign, profected whole-sign house and sign, and the supplied traditional ruler. Explain the annual emphasis through two concrete reflective practices. Use chart.natal and chart.bodies to distinguish the ruler's natal placement from its Placidus return placement when useful. Annual profections use traditional whole-sign houses; the return chart remains Placidus. Scorpio is ruled by Mars, Aquarius by Saturn and Pisces by Jupiter here; outer planets are not year lords.

If status is unavailable, explicitly state that the natal Ascendant is unavailable and no reliable profected house, sign or year lord can be supplied. Offer modest reflection without inventing replacements or substituting the return Ascendant. This honest limitation is a complete response for this section, not a failed prediction.

Use prose and, when available, a short keyValue block with the supplied age, house, sign and ruler. No upsell, claims of certainty, invented timing or missing-angle reconstruction.
