## Section brief: Houses — where the year is lived

The solar return relocates the sky into new whole-sign houses: this section maps which rooms of life hold the year's action.

Cover:
- Which whole-sign houses of the return chart hold planets (the `house` field of each entry in `chart.bodies`) — the crowded houses are where the year is lived; name them in the digest's terms.
- The house of the return Sun (`chart.sun.house`) as the anchor, and any house with three or more bodies as a hotspot.
- One house that is quiet this year, and the relief or the work of leaving it alone.

Shape: one prose block for the map, one `keyValue` block (key = house with its bodies, value = one line of meaning), one closing prose block. A `closingQuestion` is optional here.
