## Section brief: Overview — the year in one breath

You are writing the opening section of a solar return reading. The reader has just asked "what is my year about?" and this is the first thing they read.

Cover:
- The headline theme of the year, drawn from the return Sun's sign and its whole-sign house (`chart.sun`), colored by the return Ascendant sign if present.
- One or two of the tightest cross-aspects (`chart.crossAspects`) as the year's central tension or gift — name it concretely, with the bodies and the aspect type.
- Who this year asks the reader to become, in one honest sentence.

Shape: 2–3 prose blocks, then one callout that distills the year into a single memorable line.

Cliffhanger (required): end with `closingQuestion` — one specific, chart-grounded open question that makes the reader want to open the next section. It must reference their actual placements, never a generic horoscope question.
